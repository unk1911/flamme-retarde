# Changelog

All notable changes to this project. Format loosely follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

`flamme-retarde.html` is a build artifact — it is regenerated by
`python3 build.py` and committed, so every release entry implies a rebuild.
`build/payload/` is committed too, so the game builds without re-running the
geodata pipeline.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-07-27

First public release. Live at
[flamme-retarde.edeliverables.com](https://flamme-retarde.edeliverables.com/).

### The world

- **Šibenik, from public data rather than from imagination.** AWS Terrarium
  elevation tiles at z=14 and five Overpass queries (coastline, land cover,
  buildings, roads, named landmarks) baked by `tools/bake.py` into a 6 MB
  payload: a 2048² height field packed 16-bit across the red and green channels
  of a PNG, a cover raster, and gzipped JSON for the town. Decoded in the
  browser through a canvas and `DecompressionStream`; nothing is fetched at run
  time.
- **13 343 real building footprints**, extruded and roofed, in two draw calls.
- **Tiled terrain LOD**, world-anchored, one `InstancedMesh` per level, with
  vertical skirts hiding the cracks between levels.
- **The sea** as a camera-centred grid under a radial exponential warp — dense
  underfoot, kilometres wide at the horizon — with noise detail chosen
  per-pixel from `fwidth`.
- **Sky, sun and haze** driven by a single hour-of-day parameter shared by
  every material in the scene.

### The fire

- **Cellular automaton on a 256² grid**, reading its fuel from the OSM land
  cover: bare karst is the natural firebreak, maquis is why the coast goes up.
  Spread is driven by wind, slope (fire runs uphill, because flames preheat the
  fuel above them), moisture and fuel load.
- **Ember spotting.** The old town cannot be reached on foot from the ignition
  point above Jadrija — the channel is in the way — so the only way the
  cathedral burns is by embers jumping the water. The whole mission is shaped
  around that one fact.
- Instanced flame and smoke billboards with their shape written in the fragment
  shader.

### Flying

- **Canadair CL-415**, lofted procedurally from an airfoil section, in the real
  livery: yellow hull, red wingtips with the chevron run-in, tricolour
  cheatline, šahovnica on the fin. Ailerons, elevator, rudder, flaps, gear,
  scoop probes and drop doors all animate.
- Six tonnes of water in the tank makes it a different aeroplane — heavy on the
  climb out of a scoop, light the moment the doors open.
- Scooping needs wings level, under 14 m, inside a speed window, over a run of
  clear water ahead. Dropping leads the target by the fall time.

### The other three

- **Three AI wingmen** that genuinely fight the fire — find a scoop run, fill,
  cross, and drop on the leading edge nearest the town. Verified delivering
  38 000–56 000 litres per run and putting the fire out unaided.
- Radio chatter, in Croatian.

### The intro

- **A nine-beat cinematic**: the war years, the M-87 Orkan over Zagreb on
  2 May 1995, the submunitions Croatians called *jinglebells*, the ICTY
  finding, thirty summers of thyme growing over what did not go off, the same
  weapon over Kherson in 2022, and then Šibenik at 41 degrees with the *lebić*
  blowing on shore.
- **Nine painted panels**, generated with Gemini 2.5 Flash Image from the
  reference photographs (`tools/gen_panels.py`), cross-fading over the live 3-D
  on a slow push so the film cuts between painting and engine. Two beats are
  deliberately panel-free, because thirty years of sun racing over the real
  karst is the best thing the 3-D does.
- 288 instanced bomblets with their ribbons, roughly one in twenty a dud.

### Landmarks

- **Four buildings modelled in Blender** rather than generated:
  St James' Cathedral (trefoil front, barrel vault, dome on an octagonal drum,
  three apses and an abbreviated frieze of the seventy-one heads), the fortress
  of St Nicholas, the fortress of St Michael, and the Jadrija lighthouse.
- Baked to a compact binary blob — position, normal, colour, index — so there
  is no glTF parser in the bundle. `build/landmarks.blend` is kept for hand
  editing.
- OSM footprints inside a landmark's radius are skipped, so there is no
  extruded box sitting inside the cathedral.

### Vegetation

- **Instanced Aleppo pine, cypress, olive and maquis scrub**, placed from the
  same cover map the fire reads — so a tree standing in a burning cell chars
  and collapses as that cell's fuel goes.
- Deterministic per 512 m tile from a positional hash, so a tree is always in
  the same place; everything visible repacks into four instance buffers.
- Density is adjustable, and fades out at the draw radius rather than ending in
  a visible ring of forest.

### Audio

- Everything synthesised: pink-noise engine and airflow beds, blade-pass
  oscillators at four times shaft speed, scoop spray, fire crackle, stall horn,
  radio squelch.
- **Inharmonic bell partials** for the bomblets, which is where the name comes
  from.
- **Convolution reverb** whose impulse response is built at load time — a few
  discrete slap-backs off hillsides at plausible distances, then a decaying
  noise tail.
- A bowed-string bed under the intro in three colours, distant shelling,
  cicadas, and a four-layer detonation.

### Fixed

- **Banking right turned the aeroplane left.** The sign of the bank-to-turn
  coupling was inverted, so every input fought you. This was the single largest
  reason the aircraft was hard to fly.
- **The auto-leveller rolled you further over**, not upright — also a sign
  error, and it only engaged inside a 0.06 stick deadzone that an accumulating
  mouse stick could effectively never reach.
- **The virtual stick never re-centred.** Mouse motion accumulated into it and
  stayed, so letting go of the mouse left the aeroplane rolling.
- **A scoop run could not be completed.** The braking from taking water aboard
  exceeded available thrust, so speed decayed below the minimum scoop speed and
  the probes cut out part-way through every run.
- **The map edge could stall you.** The boundary force pushed straight inward,
  which for an aeroplane pointed out to sea is an airbrake; it is now a lateral
  force that turns without slowing.
- **The intro ran on the frame loop's clamped `dt`**, so on a machine rendering
  at 20 fps every beat played at a third speed. It runs on the wall clock now.
- Coastline flood fill leaked across the whole map (the DEM carries no
  bathymetry, so "below sea level" seeds landed on the coastal strip);
  fixed by classifying connected components by mean height with landmark
  anchors.
- The map border welded every island to the mainland; fixed by replicating rows
  and columns from inside the frame.
- Sea cross-hatch banding, from four separate causes: per-axis exponential warp
  making 17 km × 1 cm slivers, noise-hash precision loss at world scale,
  grazing-angle aliasing, and a shore-distance channel that was zero across the
  entire Adriatic.
- The town rendered near-black: OSM way winding is arbitrary, so half the walls
  had inward normals.
- Over-exposure across every lit surface — the diffuse term was missing its
  1/π.
- The fire would not take: the ignition point was in the sea.
- The wingmen never scooped (a terrain-avoidance floor pushed them off the
  water during a scoop run) and then never dropped (re-reading the hottest cell
  every frame gives a target that moves faster than a 300 m turn radius can
  follow).
- The frame-rate readout was reporting `1/dt` with `dt` clamped, so it always
  said 20.

### Added along the way

- `Z` holds the wings level, `T` is an autopilot that flies to the water or to
  the fire and hands the scoop and the drop back to you, the arrow keys give a
  held input for anyone who dislikes a springing mouse stick, and `M` opens a
  settings panel (mouse sensitivity, stability assist, vegetation, FOV,
  exposure) that persists across sessions.
- The virtual stick is drawn on the HUD, because a pointer-locked mouse gives
  you no other way to know where your own input is.
- `tools/shoot.mjs` drives software-GL headless Chrome over CDP; `window.__fr`
  exposes `fastForward(secs)` to step the simulation without rendering, and
  `look()` to pin the camera for a screenshot.
- `build.py` runs `node --check` on the concatenated app before shipping —
  worth the second it costs, because a stray backtick inside a GLSL template
  literal silently ends the literal and shows up only as a blank page.
