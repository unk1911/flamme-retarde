Bundle 1 — sensation (src/49-revsense.js)

 2. Ice: a cube from the bar cooler down your spine; shiver/squirm; alternated with spanks.
 3. Clothes pegs: a row along the backs of your thighs/arms, flicked off one by one with the crop.
 4. Rhythm: she hums and spanks on the beat, speeding up; you must hold still.

Bundle 2 — control and endurance (src/49-revendure.js)

Bundle 3 — power (src/49-revpower.js)

11. Lines: repeat "I'll be good for you" ten times in rhythm; a stumble restarts.
12. Her boot: ordered down to kneel at her feet and kiss the toe of her boot.

Bundle 4 — restraint plus (extends 49-revbind.js after B merges)

14. Hogtie lite: wrists behind, ankles tied, rocked onto your side, turned over by her.
15. Short leash: the leash clipped short to the cot frame or wall hook — kneel or crouch only.

Bundle 5 — her side (src/49-revdeck.js)

17. The deck: she draws a card off the shelf and it picks what comes next — genuinely random.
